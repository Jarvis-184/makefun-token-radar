# Token Radar: Farm Playbook

This playbook is a reader's guide to the Token Radar board, which tracks 200 pre-token crypto protocols ranked by implied value. All implied values are model estimates and do not represent guaranteed market prices or earnings.

## How to Read the Board
Each row on the Token Radar board represents a protocol currently in its pre-token phase.

1.  **Rank**: The protocol's position based on its model-calculated implied value.
2.  **Protocol**: The name and primary category (e.g., L2, DeFi, Restaking).
3.  **Implied Value**: A model estimate of the total potential value of a future airdrop or token launch based on funding, TVL, and social metrics.
4.  **Farm Steps**: A concise list of actions required to qualify for potential incentives.

## Farming Plans by Effort Tier
Select a strategy that matches your available time and capital.

### Tier 1: Low Effort (Passive)
*   **Focus**: Maximum 1-2 actions per protocol.
*   **Actions**: Bridge assets once, provide liquidity (LP) to a stable pool, or follow social accounts.
*   **Target**: The top 10 protocols by implied value.
*   **Time**: < 1 hour per week.

### Tier 2: Medium Effort (Active)
*   **Focus**: Weekly interaction with core features.
*   **Actions**: Use DEX aggregators, perform 3-5 swaps per month, and participate in "Quest" platforms (e.g., Galxe, Layer3).
*   **Target**: The top 50 protocols.
*   **Time**: 3-5 hours per week.

### Tier 3: High Effort (Industrial)
*   **Focus**: Daily engagement and advanced technical tasks.
*   **Actions**: Running testnet nodes, providing specialized liquidity, and maintaining multi-chain activity.
*   **Target**: All 200 protocols on the board.
*   **Time**: 15+ hours per week.

*   **Wednesday**: Check for "Snapshot" rumors. Focus on protocols in the top 10 that haven't had a social update in 30 days.
*   **Friday**: Execute routine volume swaps on L2s and L3s to maintain "active wallet" status.
*   **Sunday**: Review TVL (Total Value Locked) trends to ensure capital is deployed in high-growth protocols.

## Sybil-Risk Checklist
A "Sybil" is a single user operating multiple wallets to gain an unfair share of rewards. Protocols use filters to exclude these users. Avoid these red flags:
*   [ ] **CEX Linking**: Do not fund multiple wallets from the same Centralized Exchange account.
*   [ ] **Sequential Timing**: Do not perform the exact same steps on multiple wallets within minutes of each other.
*   [ ] **Circular Funding**: Do not send funds directly between your own farming wallets.
*   [ ] **Low Balance**: Ensure wallets maintain a "dust" buffer of at least 0.01 ETH or equivalent.
*   [ ] **Zero Socials**: Ensure wallets are linked to unique Discord/Twitter accounts where required.

## Glossary of Terms
1.  **Airdrop**: A distribution of tokens to early users or contributors.
2.  **Points**: Off-chain metrics used by protocols to track user loyalty before a token launch.
3.  **TVL**: Total Value Locked; the amount of capital deposited in a protocol.
4.  **Sybil**: An entity creating multiple identities to manipulate a system.
5.  **Snapshot**: A record of blockchain state at a specific time, used to determine eligibility.
6.  **Testnet**: A secondary blockchain used for testing features without real money.
7.  **Quest**: A structured set of tasks provided by a protocol to encourage usage.
8.  **Bridge**: A tool to move assets from one blockchain to another.
9.  **Restaking**: Re-deploying staked assets (like ETH) to secure additional networks (e.g., EigenLayer).
10. **L2 (Layer 2)**: A scaling solution built on top of a Layer 1 blockchain (like Ethereum).
11. **Mainnet**: The live version of a blockchain where real value is exchanged.
12. **DEX**: Decentralized Exchange; a platform for swapping tokens without an intermediary.
13. **Gas**: The fee required to execute a transaction on the blockchain.
14. **Liquidity Provider (LP)**: A user who deposits assets into a pool to facilitate trading.
15. **Governance**: The process by which token holders vote on protocol changes.
16. **DAO**: Decentralized Autonomous Organization; a community-led entity.
17. **Stablecoin**: A cryptocurrency pegged to a stable asset like the US Dollar.
18. **Faucet**: A service that provides small amounts of free testnet tokens.
19. **RPC**: Remote Procedure Call; the endpoint used to connect a wallet to a blockchain.
20. **Aggregator**: A tool that finds the best price across multiple DEXs.
21. **Yield**: The return earned on deposited assets.
22. **Slippage**: The difference between the expected price and the execution price of a trade.
23. **Oracle**: A service that provides external data (like prices) to a blockchain.
24. **Wallet**: Software used to interact with the blockchain (e.g., MetaMask).
25. **Seed Phrase**: A series of words used to recover a crypto wallet.

## FAQ
1.  **Are these implied values guaranteed?**
    No, they are model estimates based on current data. Market conditions change rapidly.
2.  **How often is the board updated?**
    The Token Radar system audits the board regularly based on on-chain activity and funding news.
3.  **Which chain should I start with?**
    Most high-value protocols are currently on Ethereum L2s or emerging L1s like Solana.
4.  **Is farming free?**
    Testnet farming is free (excluding time), but Mainnet farming requires gas fees and capital.
5.  **What if a protocol isn't on the board?**
    It likely doesn't meet the model's minimum criteria for funding or social traction yet.
6.  **Can I lose money?**
    Yes. Smart contract risks, hacks, and market volatility are inherent to DeFi.
7.  **Do I need a VPN?**
    Some protocols restrict access based on geography. Check local regulations.
2.  **How do I know if a snapshot was taken?**
    Official protocol Twitter accounts or Discord announcements are the only verified sources.
9.  **What is the "best" farm right now?**
    The Rank 1 protocol on the board represents the highest model-rated opportunity.
10. **Can I farm with just $100?**
    Yes, but focus on L2s with low gas fees to avoid eroding your capital.
